# Greeting

## Purpose

A visitor opens the app and sees "Hello, World!".

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a visitor, I open the app and see "Hello, World!" as the page's main heading.
- F1.2 As a visitor, I see the greeting without signing in.

## Decisions

- The greeting text is fixed: "Hello, World!".
- The page is a single screen with no navigation or other content.

## Out of Scope

- Personalised greetings or a name field.
- Changing the greeting text or language.
